Why Sotto

The noise-cancelling mic macOS was missing

Great open-source mic cleanup exists — on Linux. On macOS the options are paid and closed, or tools that make you hand-wire a virtual cable. Sotto just works.

⏻

No setup, no cables

Ships its own Core Audio driver. You never install BlackHole or route audio by hand.

●

A strong model

DeepFilterNet3 — noticeably better than the older RNNoise most open tools use.

🔒

Truly local

No account, no sign-in, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your Mac. Ever.

★

Free & open

MIT-licensed. Use it, read it, fork it. No paywall on noise removal.

How it works

Pick it like any other mic

Choose “Sotto Microphone” in your call app. Sotto captures your real mic, cleans each 10 ms chunk on-device, and hands the clean audio to the app.

your mic  →  Sotto app (captures)  →  DeepFilterNet3 (removes noise)  →  “Sotto Microphone”  →  your call app

Install

Get Sotto

Download the installer (macOS 14+, Apple Silicon) — or build from source below. A signed installer is on the way.

# clone, build, install the driver, run the app
git clone https://github.com/naga-pavan12/sotto.git
cd sotto
make
make install   # installs the audio driver (asks for your password)
make app
open app/build/Sotto.app

Then choose “Sotto Microphone” in Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, or OBS.
